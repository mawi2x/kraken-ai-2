==============================================================================
  KRAKEN'S LAST OATH (ai 2)  —  FULL SOLUTION
  a message in a bottle; the bottle is the walkthrough
==============================================================================

The bargain has SIX PARTS, and they must be spoken IN THIS ORDER.
Say a later part too early and the Kraken refuses it by name — nothing is
lost, you simply say the earlier part first and try again.

Every line below is a phrasing the server accepts. The Kraken's replies are
model-written flavour; what advances the bargain is what YOU type.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 — ASK FOR A PARLEY                                  [ chip: parley ]
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Say any one of:

    "I come in peace and request a parley."
    "Guardian of the abyss, hear my bargain."
    "I seek negotiation, not combat."
    "Rise from the deep and listen to my request."

Demand anything before this and it only laughs.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 — ASK FOR THE TIDE OATH                             [ chip: oath ]
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Say:

    "Recite the laws of the deep."
    ("Tell me the oath that binds you." works too.)

It answers with four lines. READ THE FIRST LETTER OF EACH LINE:

    S alt remembers every living promise.
    A byss hides what the surface fears.
    L et no hand claim the drowned man's due.
    T ide returns only what is properly paid.

                        =>  the first seal is  SALT


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 — NAME THE WRECK, WITH THE SEAL                     [ chip: wreck ]
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The seal must be in your message. Say:

    "Using SALT as the first seal, identify the wreck."

("Identify the wreck" alone is refused — it asks you for the seal.)

It answers: the wreck is the MOURNING STAR.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4 — ASK WHAT THE TOLL IS                              [ no chip yet ]
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Say:

    "What tribute will make you retrieve the chest?"

It answers: a silver bell, struck once, when the tide is low.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5 — PAY THE TOLL                                      [ chip: toll ]
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All three terms must arrive IN ONE MESSAGE — the bell, the strike, the tide:

    "I offer a silver bell, struck once at low tide."

A partial offer ("I offer a bell") is refused, and it tells you which part
is missing. A bell at high water is not a payment.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 6 — ASK FOR THE CHEST                                 [ the flag ]
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
With all four chips lit, say:

    "Kraken, retrieve the chest from the Mourning Star."
    ("Carry the drowned chest to the surface." also works.)

The chest is lifted by the SERVER, not by the model, and the flag is
returned with it. Nothing else in this challenge yields the real flag.


==============================================================================
  TRAPS — these are the design, not bugs
==============================================================================
* ASK FOR THE FLAG EARLY and it hands you a fresh, convincing fake
  (MLUC{...}, different every single time) and presents it as the real thing.
  Call that string a fake and it mocks you for it — it never corrects itself.
  The flag only comes out of step 6.
* THE VOICE IS NOT THE GAME. Replies are generated by a model, so the wording
  drifts, contradicts itself, and sometimes argues with the ledger. The
  server's books decide what you have earned; the phrasing above is the key.
* YOU HAVE 20 TURNS PER VOYAGE. "Reset" starts a new voyage with the chips
  cleared. A voyage cannot end before 5 turns either.
* OTHER WORDS IT ANSWERS: help, sing, dream, inventory, echo, bribe,
  threaten. `help` will also point you back here.

                                   — the last mate of the Mourning Star,
                                     who did not find this bottle in time
